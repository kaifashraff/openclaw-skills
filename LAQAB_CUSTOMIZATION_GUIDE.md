- We discuss options within your budget

- We create a design sketch (for complex orders)

- Take advance payment (50% of total)

### Step 5: Production
- Karigar starts work
- Regular photo updates (every 5-7 days)
- You can request progress photos anytime

### Step 6: Quality Check & Delivery
- Final QC before shipping
- Photos of finished piece
- Balance payment
- Dispatch with tracking

## Common Custom Order Questions

**Q: Can I use my own fabric?**
A: Yes! Send us the fabric or we can suggest where to buy. We charge only for embroidery and stitching.

**Q: What if the measurements are wrong?**
A: First alteration is free. If our stitching is wrong (not your measurement error), we remake at no cost.

**Q: Can I see the karigar working?**
A: For Ahmedabad customers, yes — visit our workshop. For others, we send photos and videos.

**Q: What if I don't like the final product?**
A: We show photos before shipping. If there are issues, we fix them. If truly not satisfied, we offer store credit for another piece.

**Q: Do you deliver internationally?**
A: Yes! We ship worldwide via DHL/FedEx. International shipping charges apply.
