## Why WhatsApp Business?

- **India's #1 commerce channel** — customers prefer WhatsApp over email/calls
- **Instant communication** — real-time quotes, order updates
- **Catalog feature** — showcase products without a website
- **Automated responses** — reply even when sleeping
- **Business profile** — builds credibility

---

## Step 1: Download WhatsApp Business

1. Download **WhatsApp Business** app (separate from personal WhatsApp)
   - Android: Play Store
   - iPhone: App Store
   
2. Verify with same phone number you want to use for business
   - Use a **dedicated business number** (not personal if possible)
   - If using personal, inform contacts you're switching to business account

## Step 3: Create Product Catalog

### Quick Catalog Setup:

1. Open WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business Tools → Catalog
2. Tap "Add Catalog"
3. For each product, add:
   - **Photo** (from website or product shots)
   - **Name** (e.g., "Royal Gold Sherwani")
   - **Price** (₹8,500)
   - **Description** (brief, e.g., "Hand embroidered, pure silk, wedding special")

## Customer Flow on WhatsApp

```
1. Customer sees Instagram/Facebook ad
2. Clicks WhatsApp link → Opens chat with pre-filled message
3. JARVIS/Kaif responds within 1 hour
4. Share catalog or specific products
5. Customer selects product / requests custom quote
6. Provide price + timeline
7. Customer confirms with advance payment
8. Order goes into production
9. Regular updates on WhatsApp
10. Delivery + request review
11. Repeat customer / referral request
```

## Pro Tips

1. **Always respond within 1 hour** — WhatsApp algorithm prioritizes fast responders
2. **Use voice messages** — Hinglish voice notes feel more personal and trustworthy
3. **Send photos** — Real photos of products build trust
4. **Be patient** — Some customers take 3-4 days to decide
5. **Don't rush** — Hand embroidery sells itself once they understand the value
