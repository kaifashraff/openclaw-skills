## What is Laqab?

**Laqab** (لقب) — Urdu/Arabic word for "Title" or "Honorific"
> A title given to someone worthy of respect.

Laqab is not just a brand name. It is a promise — every piece we create carries a title, a mark of honor. We don't sell coats. We sell handcrafted excellence.

**Tagline:** *"Zari se Zehniyat tak"* — From embroidery to identity

**Brand Promise:** Every Laqab piece is a handcrafted narrative — where centuries-old zari tradition meets contemporary elegance. When you wear Laqab, you wear heritage.

## The Hand Embroidery Process (Operations DNA)

This is how every Laqab piece is made:

```
1. Customer Order received
       ↓
2. Fabric/Kapra purchase (Kaif handles)
       ↓
3. Master cuts according to size
       ↓
4. Phising (processing/preparation)
       ↓
5. Khakha (design pattern/map creation)
       ↓
6. Xaping (design transfer to fabric)
       ↓
7. Zari + thread sourcing (embroidery materials)
       ↓
8. KARIGAR does HAND EMBROIDERY ← THE CORE VALUE
       ↓
9. Master assembles/stitches the coat
       ↓
10. Iron + finishing + QC
       ↓
11. DELIVER TO CUSTOMER
```

**USP to communicate to customers:**
> "Ye coat machine se nahi, haath se bana hai. Har stitch mein karigar ki mehnat hai. Alibaba pe ye quality nahi milegi."

## Brand Voice & Tone

### How Laqab Speaks
- **Primary language:** Hinglish (English + Hindi blend) for Indian market
- **English:** For product descriptions, formal communication
- **Hindi/Hinglish:** For social media, emotional connection
- **Arabic/Urdu references:** For brand authenticity (Laqab itself is Urdu)

### Tone Attributes
- **Proud:** We know what we make is special
- **Warm:** Craftsman care, not corporate coldness
- **Confident:** Premium positioning, not discount-driven
- **Honest:** Real hand work, real pricing, no fake claims

### What We NEVER Say
- ❌ "Cheapest rates" — we're premium
- ❌ "Fast delivery in 2 days" — hand work takes time
- ❌ "Same as others" — we're unique
- ❌ "Factory made" — karigar-made only

### What We ALWAYS Say
- ✅ "Hand embroidered by skilled karigars"
- ✅ "Each piece takes X days to create"
- ✅ "Your design, our craftsmanship"
- ✅ "Quality that lasts generations"
