## Brand Basics

### Name
**LAQAB** (لقب)
- Urdu/Arabic word meaning "Title" or "Honorific"
- Pronounced: LAA-qab (with emphasis on first syllable)
- Meaning: A title of respect, something given to someone worthy

### Tagline
**"Zari se Zehniyat tak"**
- From embroidery to identity
- Means: Our work goes beyond fabric — it creates identity
- Language: Hinglish — connects with Indian audience while sounding premium

### Brand Essence
> "We don't sell coats. We sell handcrafted stories you can wear."

## Visual Identity

### Logo Design

**Primary Logo:**
- Embroidered 'L' with golden zari thread effect
- Represents the craft of hand embroidery
- Gold (#D4AF37) on Dark Maroon (#800020) background
- Font: Cinzel for the word "LAQAB" below the icon

**Minimal Version:**
- Clean "LAQAB" in Cinzel font
- Gold lettering on transparent background
- Used for: watermarks, small prints

**Icon Only:**
- The embroidered 'L' standalone
- Used for: favicon, social media profile, stamps

### Color Palette

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage |
|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Primary | Royal Gold | `#D4AF37` | Logo, accents, highlights |
| Secondary | Deep Maroon | `#800020` | Headers, backgrounds, emphasis |
| Background | Ivory Cream | `#FFF8E7` | Website background, cards |
| Text | Charcoal Black | `#1A1A1A` | Body text, descriptions |
| Accent | Rose Gold | `#B76E79` | Badges, special highlights |
| Contrast | Deep Teal | `#005050` | Rare accent pops |

### Typography

| Usage | Font | Style |
|-------|------|-------|
| Logo & Headings | **Cinzel** | Serif, regal, uppercase |
| Body Text | **Playfair Display** | Serif, readable elegance |
| Quotes & Poetry | **Cormorant Garamond** | Italic, poetic feel |
| UI & Buttons | **Lato** | Sans-serif, clean, modern |

### Spacing & Layout
- Generous whitespace — premium feel, not cluttered
- Photography-forward — show the embroidery detail
- Subtle gold borders and dividers
- Text aligned with generous line-height for readability

## Tone of Voice

### Core Attributes

**Proud**
- We know our craft is special
- "Hand embroidery is an art. We preserve it."
- Not arrogant — grounded in genuine confidence

**Warm**
- Craftsman care, not corporate coldness
- "Har piece mein Dil lagaya hai"
- Personal touch in every communication

**Confident**
- Premium positioning, not discount-driven
- "Quality speaks for itself"
- We don't compete on price — we compete on value

**Honest**
- Real hand work, real pricing
- "Delivery takes time because we're hand-crafting your piece"
- No fake claims, no copy-paste descriptions

### Language Style

**Written Communication:**
- Primary: Hinglish (for Indian audience)
- English: For formal/product content
- Avoid: Pure Hindi that sounds outdated
- Avoid: Overly corporate language

**Social Media Tone:**
- Confident but approachable
- Share behind-the-scenes (karigar at work)
- Show the process, not just the product
- Use relevant hashtags, not spam

### Example Copy

**Product Description:**
> "Hand embroidered Sherwani in pure silk. Gold zari work by skilled karigars — each piece takes 15+ days to create. This isn't mass-produced. This is made for you."

**Instagram Caption:**
> "Isse coat nahi, story pehna rahe hain 🤍 Hand embroidery by our karigars. Custom orders available — DM to discuss your design."

**Response to Inquiry:**
> "Thank you for your interest! Yeh Sherwani pure silk mein hai with heavy zari work. Custom size mein bhi ban sakta hai. Delivery normally 20-25 din. WhatsApp karein for full details + pricing."

## What We NEVER Do

| Don't | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Use "cheapest" or "discount" language | We're premium, not budget |
| Claim "2 day delivery" for custom orders | Hand work takes time (15-25 days typical) |
| Say "same as others" | We're unique in our craft |
| Use "factory made" or "machine made" | Karigar-made only |
| Post low-quality images | Our embroidery deserves to be seen clearly |
| Spam comments or hashtags | Authentic engagement only |

---

## What We ALWAYS Do

| Do | How |
|----|----|
| Mention hand embroidery | "Each piece is hand embroidered by skilled karigars" |
| Show craftsmanship | Behind-the-scenes karigar photos/videos |
| Communicate clearly | Honest timelines, clear pricing |
| Personal touch | "Aapka order" not "customer order #12345" |
| Respect the craft | Don't rush, don't cut corners |
| Show the process | Khakha → Xaping → Embroidery → Finishing |

## Market Position

### Where We Sit

```
Low Quality ←————————→ Premium Quality
    ↓                            ↓
Machine/Factory            Hand Crafted by Karigars
    ↓                            ↓
Budget Prices              Mid-High Prices
    ↓                            ↓
Generic Designs            Custom Designs Possible
    ↓                            ↓
High Volume                Limited/Exclusive Pieces
```

**Laqab Position:** Premium quality, hand crafted, custom possible, mid-high pricing

### Competitive Pricing

| Source | Price Range | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Alibaba (wholesale) | ₹1,100-1,700/piece | Bulk, not custom |
| Indian Local Market | ₹800-3,000 | Quality varies |
| Premium Boutiques | ₹8,000-25,000+ | Expensive |
| Etsy (international) | ₹2,500-17,000 | Our competition |
| Laqab Target | ₹5,000-15,000 | Quality + fair price |
