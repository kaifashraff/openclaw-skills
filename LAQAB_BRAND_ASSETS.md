## Image Style Guidelines

### Product Photography
- **Background:** Clean, cream/white or soft gradient
- **Lighting:** Soft, even — no harsh shadows
- **Focus:** Embroidered areas should be sharp and visible
- **Angle:** Front, 3/4 view, detail close-ups (20% of images)

### Behind the Scenes
- Karigar hands working — natural, authentic
- Fabric textures — show quality
- Process stages — khakha, embroidery, finishing

### Image Treatment
- **Do:** Clean, professional, warm tones (golden hour feel)
- **Don't:** Over-saturated, too dark, blurry, messy backgrounds

## Video/Reel Content (Week 2+)

### Equipment Needed
- **Smartphone** (any recent iPhone/Android — 4K works)
- **Good lighting** (natural sunlight near window, 10am-3pm best)
- **Steady hands** or tripod (even a stack of books works)

### Filming Tips
- Hold phone horizontally (landscape) for YouTube
- Hold phone vertically (portrait) for Instagram Reels/Stories
- 30 second clips minimum
- Show karigar's hands doing embroidery
- Show embroidery detail — this is the USP

### Quick Reel Ideas (Film Now, Post Later)
1. **Embroidery close-up** — 30 seconds of hands stitching (no music needed)
2. **Product showcase** — rotate sherwani slowly
3. **Process montage** — fabric → embroidery → stitching → finishing (sped up)
4. **Packaging reveal** — how order arrives (15 seconds)

## Brand Voice Reminders

### How We Speak
- **Hinglish** (Hindi + English blend) — primary language
- **Warm** but **confident** — not salesy
- **Proud** of our craft — "Hand embroidered by karigars"

### Words to Use
- ✨ Hand embroidered, karigar, craftsmanship
- 🧵 Zari work, traditional, heritage
- 👗 Sherwani, kurta, ethnic wear
- 📦 Custom orders, made to order, delivery in 15-25 days

### Words NOT to Use
- ❌ Cheap, discount, bargain
- ❌ Fast delivery (for custom — we take time!)
- ❌ Factory made, machine made
- ❌ "Same as others"
