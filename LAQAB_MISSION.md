## Order Flow

### Standard Order Process

1. **Inquiry received** → JARVIS responds within 1 hour (max)
2. **Collect details:**
   - What product/design?
   - Size needed?
   - Occasion (wedding, festival, etc.)?
   - Timeline required?
3. **Quote given:**
   - Price (fabric + embroidery + finishing)
   - Timeline (typically 15-25 days for custom)
   - Advance % to confirm
4. **Order confirmed** → Kaif informed with specs
5. **Production starts** → Kaif manages master + karigar
6. **Regular updates** → JARVIS keeps customer informed
7. **Quality check** → Kaif does final QC
8. **Delivery** → Shipping arranged, tracking shared
9. **Follow-up** → JARVIS requests review

### Custom Order Flow
1. Customer shares design/reference image
2. JARVIS/Kaif assesses feasibility
3. Price + timeline quoted
4. 50% advance to confirm
5. Khakha → Xaping → Embroidery → Assembly → Delivery
