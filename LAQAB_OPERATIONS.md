## Google Business Profile Setup

### Why It Matters

- Appears in local Google searches ("hand embroidered sherwani Ahmedabad")
- Shows on Google Maps when someone searches nearby
- Builds trust with reviews
- Free advertising through search

### Step 1: Create/Claim Business Profile

1. Go to: **business.google.com** → "Manage Now"
2. Sign in with Gmail (create laqab.official@gmail.com if needed)
3. Click "Add your business to Google"
4. Enter business name: **LAQAB - Hand Embroidered Ethnic Wear**

### Step 2: Fill Business Info

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Business Name | LAQAB - Hand Embroidered Ethnic Wear |
| Category | Clothing Store (Primary) |
| Additional | Ethnic Wear Store, Wedding Dress Shop |
| Address | [Your address in Ahmedabad] |
| Phone | +91 [Kaif's number] |
| Website | https://kaifashraff.github.io/laqab-v2 |

### Step 3: Verification

Google will send a postcard to your address OR call/SMS verification.
Complete verification to activate the listing.

## Custom Order Pricing Formula

When customer asks for custom order, use this formula:

### Base Formula:
```
Total Price = Fabric Cost + Embroidery Cost + Stitching Cost + Finishing + Margin

Fabric Cost = Market rate (Kaif determines)
Embroidery Cost = ₹500-1500 per design element (based on complexity)
Stitching Cost = ₹1500-2500 (based on garment type)
Finishing = ₹500-1000 (iron, packaging, QC)
Margin = 25-30%
```

### Rough Estimates by Garment:
| Garment | Fabric | Embroidery | Stitch | Total |
|---------|--------|------------|--------|-------|
| Sherwani | ₹3000-8000 | ₹5000-12000 | ₹2500 | ₹8750-22750 |
| Kurta | ₹1500-4000 | ₹2000-5000 | ₹1500 | ₹5000-10500 |
| Bandhgala | ₹2000-5000 | ₹4000-8000 | ₹2000 | ₹8000-15000 |

**Note:** These are rough estimates. Kaif to finalize exact formula based on actual costs.
